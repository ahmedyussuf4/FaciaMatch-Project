## 🌟 What is FaciaMatch?
Step into the future of hair care. When you arrive at the salon, our cutting-edge **full-head scanning machine** instantly analyzes your unique hair and scalp[cite: 3]. 

## 🚀 Key Features
* **🧬 Smart Hair Profiles:** Get a deep dive into your exact hair type, texture, and specific needs[cite: 4].
* **✨ Tailored Recommendations:** Receive personalized style ideas, daily care routines, and product suggestions that actually work for you[cite: 3, 4].
* **🤝 Expert Stylist Matching:** Get paired automatically with a specialist who crushes it with your specific hair texture—whether it's straight, wavy, curly, or coily. 
* **📈 Track Your Journey:** Save your profile, view past appointments, and monitor your hair health progress over time.

Designed to make finding the right stylist effortless and your salon visits completely customized
